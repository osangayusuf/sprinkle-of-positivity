# Sprinkle of Positivity — User Manual

The platform for the **Light Shining in the Dark** Bible study accountability community. Members join groups, read a verse each day, share a short reflection (an "insight"), and keep a streak going with the help of an accountability partner.

This manual is organised by who you are:

| 3    | **Accountability partners**                    | [Partner guide](#part-3--accountability-partner-guide)                          |
| 4    | **Admins**                                     | [Admin guide](#part-4--admin-guide)                                             |

## How the three roles fit together

| Role                       | What they do                                                                                                                                                                  | How they get it                                                                        |
| -------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Member**                 | Joins groups, shares insights, answers questions and polls, chats, earns points and certificates.                                                                             | Anyone who signs up at **Join** / **Create an account**.                               |
| **Accountability partner** | Looks after a set of participants, runs the groups they are appointed to manage (approve applicants, set the verse, post questions and polls), nudges people who fall behind. | Signs up as a partner (or applies from their profile) and is **approved by an admin**. |
| **Admin**                  | Runs the whole platform: groups, partners, daily verse, announcements, marketplace, certificates, analytics.                                                                  | Promoted by another admin on the **Users** page.                                       |

Good to know:

- A person holds **one role at a time** (member, partner or admin).
- An admin can do everything a partner or member can inside any group.
- Only an **approved** partner can be appointed to manage a group.
- The "day" always changes at **midnight, Lagos time (WAT)**, wherever you are.

## Finding your way around

**Members and partners** use a bottom bar on a phone:

| Tab             | Goes to                                        |
| --------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| **Home**        | Verse of the day, your groups, featured offers |
| **Leaderboard** | Points ranking                                 |
| **Groups**      | Your groups and groups to discover             |
| **Profile**     | Your points, certificates, settings, log out   |

Visitors who are not logged in see **Home, Groups, Market, Log in**.

On the **Home** screen, the top-right icons show: a **people icon** (partners: "My participants"), a **shield icon** (admins: "Go to admin portal"), and a **bell** (notifications; a dot means unread).

**Admins** use a side menu in the admin portal: Dashboard, Analytics, Groups, Today's verse, Send announcement, Marketplace, Users (and Certificates and Participants from the Dashboard cards).

# Part 1 — Getting started (everyone)

## 1.1 Browse without an account

Visitors can look around before signing up.

1. Open the site. You see the landing page. Use **Log in** or **Join** at the top, or the menu on a phone.
2. Open **Groups** to see every group, with its purpose and number of members.
3. Tap a group to read its page. Public groups also show the daily verse and insights.
4. Private groups (marked with a lock) show only the page; their verses and insights are for members.
5. Anything that takes part (join, share, comment, vote) shows _"Log in or create an account…"_ with **Log in** and **Join** buttons. After logging in you return to the same page.
6. **Market** lists current offers. **Give / Support** (linked from the landing page) shows the bank accounts you can donate to; use **Copy number** or **Copy all details**.

## 1.2 Create a member account

1. Tap **Join** at the top of the page.
2. Enter **Name**, **Email address**, **Password** and **Confirm password**.
3. Tap **Create account**.
4. Open the verification email and click the link. If it did not arrive, on the **Email verification** screen tap **Resend verification email**.
5. You are taken to profile set-up. Continue at [1.5](#15-complete-your-profile-first-login-only).

## 1.3 Create a partner account

For people who will look after participants.

1. Go to the partner sign-up page: on the member sign-up page, under the form, tap **Accountability partner? Sign up here**. (On the partner page, **Joining as a participant? Sign up here** switches back.)
2. Enter **Name**, **Email address**, **Password**, **Confirm password**.
3. Tap **Request partner account**.
4. You land on **Awaiting approval**. You cannot use the rest of the app until an admin approves you. The only thing you can do there is **Log out**.
5. When approved, log in again and you reach Home with the partner tools. If your request was declined, the screen says so and tells you to contact the programme team.

> Already a member and want to become a partner? See [2.17](#217-apply-to-become-an-accountability-partner).

## 1.4 Log in, log out, reset your password

**Log in**

1. Tap **Log in**.
2. Enter **Email address** and **Password**. Tick **Remember me** to stay signed in on this device.
3. Tap **Log in**.
4. If you turned on two-factor authentication, enter the **Authentication code** from your app. Lost your phone? Choose the recovery option and enter a **Recovery code**.
5. If you registered a passkey, you can use that instead of typing a password.

**Log out**: **Profile → Logout** (partners awaiting approval: **Log out** on the waiting screen).

**Forgot your password**

1. On the login page tap **Forgot password**.
2. Enter your **Email address** and submit.
3. Open the email and click the reset link.
4. Enter and confirm a new password.

## 1.5 Complete your profile (first login only)

Until this is done you cannot use the app. It has four short steps; use the back arrow to return to a previous step.

1. **Tell us your name** — check or change your full name → **Continue**.
2. **Share your WhatsApp number** — enter your phone number → **Continue**.
3. **What are your goals?** — tap one or more: _Know the Bible better, Get closer to God, Grow in community, Build a daily habit, Deepen my faith_ → **Continue** (at least one is needed).
4. **When is your birthday?** — choose **Day** and **Month** → **Continue**. Tap **Skip** to leave the birthday blank.

You then arrive at **Home**.

## 1.6 Install the app on your phone

The platform works as an installable web app.

1. Open the site in your phone browser (Safari on iPhone, Chrome on Android).
2. Use the browser menu: **Add to Home Screen** / **Install app**.
3. Open it from your home screen like any other app.

## 1.7 Turn on push notifications

1. Go to **Profile**.
2. Tap **Push notifications**. It reads _Off_; allow notifications when the browser asks.
3. It now reads _On_. Tap again to switch off.

If the row is missing, your browser does not support push notifications (on iPhone, install the app to the Home Screen first).

# Part 2 — Member guide

## 2.1 The Home screen

- **Verse of the day**: the platform-wide verse with its translation and copyright credit. If none is set, you see _"Your daily verse is almost here"_.
- **Your Groups**: up to three groups you have recently been active in. Tap **View all** for the full list.
- **Featured Market Place**: a swipeable row of offers. **See all** opens the Marketplace.
- Greeting and **bell** at the top.

## 2.2 Find and join a group

1. Tap **Groups** in the bottom bar.
2. **My Groups** lists groups you belong to. **Discover** lists the rest.
3. Tap a group to read its **Group purpose**, see whether it is private, and see how many members it has.
4. Tap **Join Group**.
5. Read the five commitments (follow submission guidelines; study and submit daily; tell the group or convener if you will miss a deadline; be courteous; understand that breaking the code may lead to removal). **Tick every box.**
6. Tap **Continue**. The button only works once all boxes are ticked.
7. The group page now shows **Application pending approval**.
8. A partner or admin reviews your application. When approved, the group moves into **My Groups** and you are assigned an accountability partner (you will be notified).

You can only apply once per group. If you were removed or declined, contact the programme team.

## 2.3 Read today's verse

1. Open your group and tap **Read Bible Study Insights** (members of private groups only).
2. At the top you see the **streak tracker**: _Your day X / total_, your _current streak_ and _best_, and any _restarts_. A tick marks each completed day.
3. Below it is the **Today's verse** card. If the manager has not set it yet it says _"No verse has been set for today yet."_ Check back later.

## 2.4 Share your reflection (insight)

Posting an insight is how you complete the day.

1. On the group's verse page, make sure the **Bible Study Insights** tab is selected.
2. Tap **Share your reflection**.
3. Type your reflection in the box (up to 5,000 characters).
4. _(Optional)_ tap the picture icon to attach an image (up to 5 MB). Tap the **×** on the preview to remove it.
5. Tap **Submit**.
6. You earn **+10 points**, today gets its tick on the streak tracker, and you land on your insight.

You can only post on **today's** verse. Past days are read-only.

## 2.5 Read others' insights, react and comment

1. On the **Bible Study Insights** tab, scroll the list of reflections.
2. Tap an insight (text or image) to open it with its comments.
3. **React**: tap the reaction button and choose 🔥 😊 😲 ❤️ or 🙏. Tap the same emoji again to remove it, or pick another to change it. You get one reaction per insight or comment.
4. **Comment**: type in the **Add comment** box at the bottom and tap the arrow (up to 2,000 characters). You earn **+5 points** per comment.
5. React to a comment with the reaction button under it.

## 2.6 Answer a question

Managers post questions on the **Q&A & Polls** tab.

1. Open the **Q&A & Polls** tab.
2. Tap the option you think is right. **You can answer once**, so choose carefully.
3. You earn **+5 points** for answering, plus **+15** if you were right (20 total).
4. After answering, the correct option is highlighted green and a wrong pick is highlighted red.

## 2.7 Vote in a poll

Polls look like questions but have no right answer and give no points.

1. Open the **Q&A & Polls** tab.
2. Tap your choice. The toast says _Vote saved._ and the results appear (bars with % and vote counts). Results stay hidden until you vote.
3. To change your mind, tap another option; your single vote moves.
4. When a manager closes the poll it shows **Closed** and votes can no longer change.

## 2.8 Look at past days

1. On the verse page scroll to **Past days** (the last six months).
2. Open a month, then tap a day. You see that day's verse and its insights. Each row shows the verse reference and the number of insights.
3. You can also tap a day on the streak tracker.
4. Tap **Back to today** to return.

Past days are for reading; new insights can only be added on the current day.

## 2.9 Chat with your group

1. Open your group page and tap **Group chat** (only approved members see it).
2. The chat opens at the newest message. Tap **Load earlier messages** to read history.
3. Type in **Write a message** (up to 1,000 characters). Press **Enter** to send, or **Shift + Enter** for a new line. You can also tap the send button.
4. New messages from others appear by themselves every few seconds.
5. **Delete a message**: tap the bin icon beside your own message, then **Delete message** to confirm. It is removed for everyone. Managers can delete anyone's message.

## 2.10 Your streak, restarts and certificate

- You complete a day by posting an insight on that day's verse.
- If you **miss a day that had a verse**, your run restarts at day 1. Days with no verse set do not count against you, and days before you joined never break a run.
- At 18:00 (Lagos) a reminder goes to members who have not yet posted. Your partner may also **nudge** you.
- When your run reaches the full length of the challenge, a certificate is issued automatically (the system checks daily at 01:00). You get the notification _"Your certificate is ready"_.

## 2.11 Points, levels and the leaderboard

1. Tap **Leaderboard** to see everyone ranked by points (🥇🥈🥉 for the top three; your row is highlighted).
2. Tap **View levels** to see every level, its points threshold, and which you have achieved.
3. Tap **Consistency** to see the longest current streaks and the most consistent partners.
4. When your points cross a level threshold a celebration pops up. Tap **Continue**.

See the [points table](#51-how-points-work) for how to earn them.

## 2.12 View and print your certificate

1. **Profile → Certificates**.
2. Tap a certificate (it shows the group, number of days and year).
3. Tap **Print or save PDF** and choose _Save as PDF_ in the print window.
4. Tap **Copy link** to share the public certificate page.

Anyone with the link can open the certificate. A revoked certificate shows _"This certificate is no longer valid"_.

## 2.13 Notifications

1. Tap the **bell** on Home.
2. Notifications are grouped by day; unread ones are highlighted.
3. Tap one to read it in full.

## 2.14 Marketplace

1. Tap **Market** (or **Featured Market Place → See all** on Home).
2. Browse the offers. Tap an offer's button to open its link.

## 2.15 Edit your profile

1. **Profile → Edit profile**.
2. **Profile picture**: tap **Upload photo** (or **Change photo**) and choose an image (up to 2 MB). Tap **Remove photo** to delete it.
3. Change **Name**, **Email address**, **WhatsApp number**, **Birthday** (day and month) and **Goals**. At least one goal must stay selected; a saved WhatsApp number cannot be cleared.
4. Tap **Save**.
5. If you changed your email, you may need to verify it again (**Resend the verification email**).

## 2.16 Security and appearance

**Change password**

1. Open the settings **Security** tab (you may be asked to confirm your password).
2. Enter **Current password**, **New password**, **Confirm password**.
3. Tap **Save**.

**Two-factor authentication**

1. In **Security**, find the two-factor section and tap to enable it.
2. Scan the QR code with an authenticator app, enter the code to confirm.
3. Save the **recovery codes** somewhere safe.
4. To switch off or regenerate codes, use the same section.

**Passkeys**: in **Security**, add a passkey and follow your device prompt; you can then sign in without a password. Remove one from the same list.

**Appearance**: open the **Appearance** tab and choose **Light**, **Dark** or **System** for this device.

**Delete your account**: at the bottom of **Profile settings** tap **Delete account**, enter your password and confirm. This cannot be undone.

## 2.17 Apply to become an accountability partner

1. **Profile → Become an accountability partner** (shown only if you are not already a partner or admin).
2. The row changes to **Partner application pending**.
3. An admin reviews it. You get a notification with the result. If declined, the row says _Your last application was declined. You can apply again._
4. After approval you keep your account and gain the partner tools. Your role changes from member to partner.

# Part 3 — Accountability partner guide

Everything in the member guide that applies to reading, reacting, commenting and chatting still applies. This part covers the partner-only tools. You need an **approved** partner account ([1.3](#13-create-a-partner-account)) and, to run a group, an admin must appoint you to it.

## 3.1 My participants

1. On Home, tap the **people icon** (top right).
2. At the top, three counters show how your participants are doing:
    - **Done today** (green) — posted today's insight.
    - **Yet to post** (amber) — hasn't posted yet today.
    - **Behind** (red) — lagging.
3. Each card shows the participant's name, group, status, **Day** (their place in the challenge), **Streak**, **Restarts**, and **Last post**.
4. Tap **View consistency leaderboard** to compare partners and see who needs a check-in.

New participants are assigned to you automatically (the partner with the fewest participants gets the next one), and you are notified when it happens.

## 3.2 Read a participant's insights

On their card tap **Insights** to see everything they have posted in the group, newest first. Tap one to open it with its comments.

## 3.3 Nudge a participant

1. On their card tap **Nudge**.
2. They receive _"Your partner is cheering you on"_.
3. You can nudge each participant **once per day**.

## 3.4 Reassign a participant you cannot take

1. On their card tap **Reassign**.
2. Type **why you can't take this participant**.
3. Tap **Move to another partner** (or **Cancel**).
4. The participant moves to another partner in the group. If none is available, admins are alerted so they can assign someone manually.

## 3.5 Manage a group you were appointed to

On the group page, a manager sees a **⋮ (Manage group)** icon at the top right.

**Approve or reject applications**

1. Tap **⋮** → **Pending applications**.
2. For each applicant tap **Approve** or **Reject**.
3. Approved members are assigned to a partner automatically.

**Remove a member**

1. **⋮** → **Members**.
2. Tap **Remove** beside the member.

**Review a member's insights**: **⋮** → **Members** → **Insights**.

## 3.6 Set today's verse for your group

1. Open the group → **Read Bible Study Insights**.
2. Tap **Set today's verse** (or **Edit today's verse** to change it).
3. Choose a **Version** (e.g. BSB).
4. Choose a **Book**, then **Chapter**, then **From verse**. Choose **To verse** for a range.
5. The text fills in automatically in **Verse text**. You can still edit it.
6. _(Optional)_ add an **Image**.
7. Tap **Save**.

If the Bible versions list cannot load, you will see a plain **Reference** box instead: type something like `John 3:16` and tap **Look up**, or type the verse text yourself.

If the chosen version is not available the platform falls back to a public-domain translation and tells you; no translation credit is saved then.

## 3.7 Post a question or a poll

On the **Q&A & Polls** tab (today only):

1. Tap **Add a question or poll**.
2. Choose **Question** (quiz with a right answer, gives points) or **Poll** (opinion, no points).
3. Type the question (up to 1,000 characters).
4. Fill in at least two options, up to six. Use **+ Add option** to add more, **×** to remove one.
5. For a **Question**, tick the radio button beside the correct option.
6. Tap **Post question** or **Post poll**.

**Edit**: tap the pencil on the item, change it, tap **Save changes**. Items that already have answers cannot be edited.

**Delete**: tap the bin, confirm **Delete question / Delete poll**. If members have answered, their votes are removed and points earned from it are reversed.

**Close / reopen a poll**: tap **Close poll** (or **Reopen poll**) under the poll. As a manager you can see the results at any time.

## 3.8 Moderate the chat

Open **Group chat**. Next to any member's message tap the bin → **Delete message** to remove it for everyone.

## 3.9 Notifications you will receive

- _New participant assigned_ when someone joins your caseload.
- _Participants yet to post_ — a daily summary in the evening.
- Platform announcements from admins.

# Part 4 — Admin guide

Admins reach the portal from the **shield icon** on Home, or by going to `/admin/dashboard`. The **Dashboard** shows cards for each area with counts (groups, active listings, users, pending partners, restarts this week).

## 4.1 Analytics

1. **Analytics** in the side menu.
2. Pick the period: **Last 7 / 30 / 90 days** (the range buttons).
3. Read the tiles: Unique visitors, Guest visitors, Signed-in visitors, Logins, New sign-ups, Active members.
4. Below: the **Unique visitors per day** chart (hover for the numbers), **Top pages**, **Member activity** (insights, comments, reactions, quiz answers, group applications), **Recent logins**, **Top referrers**.

## 4.2 Manage users and roles

1. **Users** in the side menu.
2. The table lists Name, Email, Points and Role.
3. **Change a role**: use the dropdown (**Member / Accountability partner / Admin**). Setting **Accountability partner** approves them as a partner immediately.
4. **Approve or reject a partner request**: people who signed up or applied as partners show _Awaiting approval_. Tap **Approve** or **Reject**. Approval turns an applicant who was a member into a partner.
5. Rules: you cannot remove your own admin access, and you cannot change a partner's role while they still manage groups (remove them from those groups first).

## 4.3 Create a group

1. **Groups → New group**.
2. Fill in:
    - **Name**
    - **Purpose** (what the group is for)
    - **Duration (days)** — length of the challenge
    - **Start date**
    - **Accountability partners** — tick at least one approved partner. Participants are shared between the ones you pick. If the list is empty, approve a partner first on **Users**.
    - **Private group** — tick if only members should read verses and insights. Anyone can still see the group and apply.
    - **Cover image** (up to 2 MB)
3. Tap **Create group**.

## 4.4 Edit, archive or delete a group

1. **Groups**, then **Edit** on the group.
2. Change any field. **Status** can be **Active** or **Archived**. Leave **Cover image** blank to keep the current one.
3. Tap **Save changes**.
4. To delete: on the Groups list tap **Delete** → **Delete group**. This permanently removes the group, its members, verses, insights and quizzes.

## 4.5 Assign participants to partners

1. **Groups → Partners** on a group.
2. For each participant, pick a partner in the dropdown.
3. The partner is notified straight away.

Normally assignment is automatic (least-loaded partner). Use this page to rebalance or to place someone who is _Unassigned_.

## 4.6 Approve and manage applicants to a group

Admins can do everything a manager can in any group: open the group → **⋮ Manage group** to approve/reject applications and remove members, set the group's verse, and post questions and polls ([3.5–3.7](#35-manage-a-group-you-were-appointed-to)).

## 4.7 Set the platform's daily verse (Home screen)

**One day**

1. **Today's verse** in the side menu.
2. Pick the **Date** (today or later).
3. Choose **Version → Book → Chapter → From verse (→ To verse)**; the **Verse text** fills in. Edit if needed.
4. _(Optional)_ add an **Image**.
5. Tap **Save**.

**Many days at once**

1. **Set many at once** (on the Today's verse page).
2. For each row choose a **Date**, pick the passage, check the **Verse text**, add an optional **Image**.
3. **Add another row** for more days, **×** to remove a row.
4. Tap **Save all rows**.

**By CSV**

1. On the same page, under **Or import a CSV**, choose a `.csv` or `.txt` file with columns `date, reference, text` (a header row is fine).
2. Tap **Upload**.

**History**: **View history** lists every verse ever set; **Edit** appears for today and future dates.

## 4.8 Send an announcement

1. **Send announcement**.
2. Enter a **Title** (e.g. _Weekly Fast Alert!_) and a **Message** (up to 2,000 characters).
3. Tap **Send**. It is delivered to every user's notifications (and by push to those who enabled it).

## 4.9 Marketplace listings

**Create**

1. **Marketplace → New listing**.
2. Fill **Title**, **Description**, **Button label** (default _Read More_), **Button link** (a full URL), **Position** (lower shows first), and an **Image** (up to 2 MB).
3. Tap **Create listing**.

**Edit**: **Edit** on a row; you can also set **Starts** / **Ends** dates and **Status** (**Active** / **Inactive**). Tap **Save changes**.

**Remove**: tap **Remove** on a row.

## 4.10 Certificates

Certificates are issued **automatically** every day at 01:00 to members who completed the full challenge.

1. **Certificates** (Dashboard card) lists challenge groups with their dates, members and certificates issued. Tap **Manage**.
2. For each member you see **Days** completed, **Streak (best)** and **Status**.
3. **Issue** (for a member the system judged ineligible): tap **Issue**, enter the **Reason for the override**, tap **Issue certificate**.
4. **Edit name** to fix a spelling; the link stays the same. Tap **Save name**.
5. **Revoke**: enter the **Reason for revoking** and confirm. The public page then says the certificate is no longer valid (the reason is not shown to the public) and it will not be re-issued.
6. **Restore** brings a revoked certificate back.
7. **View** opens the certificate.

## 4.11 Watch participants

**Participants** (Dashboard card) shows _All participants_ across all partners, with the partner's name on each card. You can **Nudge** any participant and open their **Insights**. The card also reports restarts in the last 7 days.

## 4.12 Your admin notifications

You receive an alert when a participant ends up with no partner (_"Participant needs a partner"_). Resolve it via [4.5](#45-assign-participants-to-partners).

# Part 5 — Reference

## 5.1 How points work

| Action                      | Points              |
| --------------------------- | ------------------- |
| Share an insight            | +10                 |
| Post a comment              | +5                  |
| Answer a question           | +5                  |
| Answer a question correctly | +15 more (20 total) |
| Vote in a poll              | 0                   |
| Reactions, chat messages    | 0                   |

## 5.2 Levels

| Level     | Points needed |
| --------- | ------------- |
| Amateur   | 500           |
| Novice    | 1,000         |
| Leader    | 2,000         |
| Senior    | 10,000        |
| Moderator | 20,000        |
| Master    | 200,000       |
| Professor | 500,000       |
| Noble     | 5,000,000     |

## 5.3 Streak and certificate rules

- A day is completed by posting an insight on that day's group verse.
- A missed day that had a verse restarts your run at day 1 (counted as a restart).
- Days with no verse set, and days before you joined, never break a run.
- A certificate needs one unbroken run as long as the group's challenge length. It may therefore be earned after the group's original end date.

## 5.4 Notifications you may receive

| Notification                      | Who                        | When                                         |
| --------------------------------- | -------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| _Don't miss today_                | Members who haven't posted | Daily at 18:00 Lagos                         |
| _Participants yet to post_        | Partners                   | Daily, same time                             |
| _Your partner is cheering you on_ | Participant                | When a partner nudges                        |
| _New participant assigned_        | Partner                    | When a participant is assigned or reassigned |
| _Participant needs a partner_     | Admins                     | When no partner is available                 |
| _Your certificate is ready_       | Member                     | When a certificate is issued                 |
| Announcements                     | Everyone                   | When an admin sends one                      |
| Level-up pop-up                   | Member                     | When points cross a level                    |

# Part 6 — Troubleshooting

| Problem                                      | What to do                                                                                                                                                   |
| -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| I keep being sent to "Complete your profile" | Finish all four steps in [1.5](#15-complete-your-profile-first-login-only).                                                                                  |
| I can't see Bible Study Insights             | The group is private and you are not an approved member yet. Join and wait for approval.                                                                     |
| There is no "Share your reflection" button   | You must be an approved member, and the group must have a verse set for today.                                                                               |
| "No verse has been set for today yet"        | The manager hasn't set it. Ask them, or check later.                                                                                                         |
| I can't edit a question                      | It already has answers. Delete and repost it instead.                                                                                                        |
| My partner account says "Awaiting approval"  | An admin has not approved it yet. Contact the programme team.                                                                                                |
| The verse picker only shows a text box       | Bible versions couldn't load. Type the reference and use **Look up**, or type the verse text.                                                                |
| I was not notified                           | Check **Profile → Push notifications** is _On_ and that browser notifications are allowed. In-app notifications are always under the bell.                   |
| My streak restarted                          | A day with a verse passed without an insight from you ([5.3](#53-streak-and-certificate-rules)).                                                             |
| Certificate missing                          | It is issued automatically once your unbroken run reaches the challenge length; ask an admin to review it under **Certificates** if you believe you qualify. |
| I can't change a partner back to member      | Remove them from the groups they manage first ([4.2](#42-manage-users-and-roles)).                                                                           |

### Notes for the programme team (setting up the platform)

- Set `YOUVERSION_APP_KEY` on the server so the version/book/chapter/verse pickers work. Without it the platform falls back to a plain reference box and a public-domain translation.
- Notifications, reminders and certificates rely on the scheduled task (cron running `php artisan schedule:run` every minute) because the server has no always-on queue worker.
